StatCalculationRaw
What are 100%, 70% and 20%? They describe how a piece splits its stat budget between substats. A piece’s strongest substat gets the full budget for its slot (the table value × the slot share, rounded), which is the 100%. The second substat is about 70% of that rounded number, and a small third one (mostly Direct Hit on healer gear) is about 20%, each rounded again. Example: a head piece at item level 130 has a budget of 31, so its second substat is 31 × 70% ≈ 22 and its third is 31 × 20% ≈ 6. The game files store only each item’s final stats, so these shares are a pattern measured from real gear, not a published rule. They reproduce about 97–99% of real pieces exactly (see “Checked against real gear” in the Sync caps view). A piece with two substats just leaves the 3rd box on “none”. The Item pool and Optimizer use each item’s real stats and don’t depend on these shares.

A stat on a piece = column value at the piece’s item level × slot share, rounded; the 2nd and 3rd substats are then 70% and 20% of the first, rounded again. The table is not linear: it is steep around 135–150 and flattens after, which is why very high item levels buy far more raw stats than you can use under a sync cap.

Model assumptions: every stat on a synced-down piece is clamped to the value a 100%-share stat on a same-slot piece at the cap item level would have; stats on pieces at or below the cap are untouched; materia is ignored on synced-down gear. The main-stat and Vitality clamp is inferred and not confirmed in-game. Real pieces have fixed substat pairs; the formula reproduces about 97–99% of real pieces exactly.
