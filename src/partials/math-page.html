How sync works

  • Sync caps stats instead of scaling gear down. A stat above its slot’s cap drops to the cap. A stat below the cap stays the same. (For the main stat and Vitality this is a guess; the Wiki covers substats only.)
  • A piece at or below the synced item level is untouched and keeps its materia.
  • A piece above the synced item level loses all its materia.
  • Both substats on a piece have the same cap. The 70% substat reaches the cap when the piece is about 120 item levels above the synced item level.
  • Overgeared or melded depends on the gap. From about 95 item levels above the synced item level, overgeared gear has more substat points than melded gear at the synced item level. Below that gap, the melded gear has more.
    • At levels 50 and 60, where materia adds little, the gap is much smaller.
  • Every job gets the same caps. A few are lower (90%): Vitality on healer and caster gear, and the main stat and Vitality on gear for every job.
  • Level sync lowers your character level. Item level sync caps your gear’s item level. A duty can use both; the item level cap wins, up to the best gear made for the synced level.

Sources: FFXIV Wiki, Item Level and Level Sync; game data from ffxiv-datamining.
