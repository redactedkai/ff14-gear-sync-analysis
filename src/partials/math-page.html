How gear stats work

  • The item level and slot set a budget for each piece’s stats: the main stat (like Strength or Mind), Vitality, and sub-stats (like Critical Hit or Tenacity).
  • The main stat and Vitality generally hold 100% of their budget, except Vitality on magical ranged and healer gear (90%).
  • Primary sub-stats can hold 100% of that budget, and secondary sub-stats can hold 70%.
  • Notable exceptions:
    • Some all-job earrings / rings have their main stat and Vitality capped at 90% when synced.
    • Some level 50 healer gear has a tertiary Direct Hit substat accounting for 20-25% of that budget (remapped from Accuracy back in 4.0).

How stat syncing works

  • Level sync lowers your character’s level, and item level sync caps your gear’s item level.
  • A synced piece’s stats are clamped to a maximum based on its slot and synced item level. Stats below the limit are unaffected.
  • Pieces synced down lose their materia, while pieces at or below the item level limit are unaffected.
  • Level sync alone syncs pieces made for a higher level to a set item level (level 80: item level 530).
  • When both level and item level sync are active, the item level cap decides.
  • Gear that scales with the wearer’s level (such as Menphina’s Earring) always syncs to the duty’s level.

Sources: FFXIV Wiki, Item Level and Level Sync; game data from ffxiv-datamining.
